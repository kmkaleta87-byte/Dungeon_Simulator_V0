DUNGEON SIMULATION - ART ASSET CHECKLIST
========================================

File format: PNG. Keep transparent backgrounds for isolated sprites and icons.
Use the exact paths and lowercase filenames below. Filenames use kebab-case.
Suggested sprite framing: one object/enemy centered with some transparent padding.
Weapon art is displayed beside the player/loadout, not as a player character sprite.

RARITY DESIGN
-------------
Each weapon checklist line is tagged with its exact in-game rarity. Armor rarity
is tagged by material/set. Keep the filenames unchanged; make higher-rarity art
feel more distinctive through stronger silhouettes, detail, materials, and accents.
Use the game's established rarity colors as accents where they suit the design:
Common white, Uncommon light green, Rare dark blue, Epic purple, Legendary light
orange/gold, Mythic red, Divine pale turquoise. Higher rarity should read as more
special, not merely as the same item with a brighter tint.

Potion rarity varies when dropped. Use one base potion sprite plus the matching
rarity frame/accent rather than drawing seven near-identical full potion sprites.
Small Health Potion in the starting/treasure rooms is Common; the weapon chest's
Large Health Potion is Rare.

FOLDER GUIDE
------------
Weapons/Warrior   Individual warrior weapons
Weapons/Archer    Individual bows and crossbows
Weapons/Mage      Individual wands and staffs
Armor             Armor piece sprites, grouped by material set
Enemies/Races     Regular race/class enemy sprites
Enemies/Bosses    Named boss sprites
Enemies/Overlays  Reusable elite/glass-cannon visual overlays
Items/Potions     Potion sprites
Items/Materials   Reusable material/arrow sprites
Rooms             Room illustrations or room thumbnails
UI                Reusable interface icons and frames

NOTES
-----
- Weapon names above follow the current weapon catalogs. Use one distinct sprite per item.
- Armor is generated from seven material sets across five equipment slots.
- Enemy art is grouped by normal, elite, and boss rank rather than item rarity;
  bosses are red and elites purple in the current game UI.
- Rarity does not apply to rooms, enemy races, effect icons, or element icons.
- ArmorSlot.Head is named "helmet" here; Feet is "boots"; Legs is "leggings".
- Regular enemies have no Archer class for specters or vampires, no Mage werewolf,
  and golems/slimes currently use the listed limited forms.
- Bosses use their own named art; glass-cannon bosses may reuse the matching boss
  art with the glass-cannon overlay.
- More items/rooms may be added later; this is the current game's asset inventory.
- Region themes, Zombie sprites, and the floor-20 milestone boss are planned;
  only the Undead example is named, and the boss identity remains undecided.
