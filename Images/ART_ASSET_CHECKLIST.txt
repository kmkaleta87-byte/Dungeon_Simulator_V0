DUNGEON SIMULATION - ART ASSET CHECKLIST
========================================

Painted raster art uses PNG; custom weapon, armor, potion, and enemy vectors use transparent SVGs. Keep transparent backgrounds for isolated sprites and icons.
Use the exact paths and lowercase filenames below. Filenames use kebab-case.
Suggested sprite framing: one object/enemy centered with some transparent padding.
Weapon art is displayed beside the player/loadout, not as a player character sprite.

RARITY DESIGN
-------------
Weapon art uses one clear base silhouette per type across all rarity tiers; rarity
is communicated by the game's labels and stats. Armor icons vary by set material.

Potion rarity varies when dropped. Use one shared health/mana sprite through
Legendary; Mythic and Divine health/mana potions each have distinct art.
Small Health Potion in the starting/treasure rooms is Common; the weapon chest's
Large Health Potion is Rare.

NOTES
-----
- Weapon names above follow the current weapon catalogs. Use one distinct sprite per item.
- Armor is generated from seven material sets across five equipment slots.
- Enemy art is grouped by normal, elite, and boss rank rather than item rarity;
  bosses are red and elites purple in the current game UI.
- Rarity does not apply to rooms, enemy races, effect icons, or element icons.
- ArmorSlot.Head is named "helmet" here; Feet is "boots"; Legs is "leggings".
- Regular enemies have no Archer class for specters or vampires, no Mage werewolf,
  and golems/slimes currently use the listed limited forms.
- Bosses use their own named art; glass-cannon bosses may reuse the matching boss
  art with the glass-cannon overlay.
- More items/rooms may be added later; this is the current game's asset inventory.
- The four current regions cycle every 20 floors: Undead Catacombs, Goblin
  Warrens, Haunted Hollows, and Feral Stone Depths. Region art paths above match
  the browser build; the milestone boss is The Threshold Warden.
